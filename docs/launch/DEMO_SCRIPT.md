# Demo script

## 60-second screen recording (for creators, admins and your own posts)
Record your phone or computer screen. Use the test files in `tests/fixtures/` plus a few of your own
**non-sensitive** files. Never show real personal documents.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over / caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5s | The home page | "Partner visa evidence is a mess of photos, statements and screenshots. ImmiAccount wants each file under 5 MB." |
| 5–15s | Builder → drag in ~30 files, including an iPhone HEIC photo and a "secured" bank PDF | "Drop everything in, even iPhone photos and locked bank statements." |
| 15–25s | Sort step: files bunched by type; choose a group for "Bank, loan and pay documents" in one tap; tap "Check dates" | "It bunches files by type, and you choose where each type goes. Suggested dates are only printed once you've checked them." |
| 25–40s | Build step → Build → "Your pack is ready: all under 5 MB" | "One tap builds date-ordered PDFs, each with a contents page, plus an index." |
| 40–50s | Turn on Airplane mode and build again; it still works | "It runs entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Turn on Airplane mode and see." |
| 50–60s | The pricing card | "Free for up to 10 files. A$29 once for a full pack." Then show your live URL on screen |

## Live conversation (if someone wants to chat)
1. **Ask first:** "How far along are you, and what's the hardest part of the uploads?" Listen. Don't pitch yet.
2. **Show their problem being solved.** Share your screen and use test files that resemble their situation
   (too big, HEIC, locked PDF, 200 files).
3. **Address trust:** the Airplane-mode check, and the fact that their files are never sent to you.
4. **Be clear about limits:** "It doesn't tell you what evidence you need. That's for Home Affairs or a registered agent."
5. **Ask for the sale plainly:** "It's free to try. If your pack is over 10 files it's A$29 once, and if you change your
   mind there's a full refund within 30 days. Want to give it a go now?"
6. **Afterwards:** log the conversation in `tracker.csv`, including objections word for word.
