## Key evidence

- [COMPLAINT] Xero users update hundreds of repeating invoices by hand at each price rise (one cites 600);
  no export of repeating invoices. Ideas 45587665, 49331243, 44960668 (vote counts unavailable).
  [SPEND] a developer advertises a $29/mo tool in-thread (vendor claim).
- [FACT] Xero API pricing since 2 Mar 2026: Starter free up to 5 connected orgs; Core A$35/mo up to 50 orgs.
  — developer.xero.com/pricing ; truto.one/blog/xero-api-pricing-changes-2026-costs-tiers-and-how-to-minimize-egress/
- [FACT] Xero attachment bulk export idea ~673–697 votes; Xero 20 Nov 2025 "not in our plans"; already served by
  Boxkite (from US$6/org/mo), Numerint, FileXtract, Control-C, Ledgerscope, Synkle, OSS script.
- [SPEND] Airtasker posted budgets for receipt data entry: A$35, A$50, A$70, A$100, A$150 (≈800 receipts).
  Upwork bank-statement→Excel packages from US$7.50; a Freelancer job drew 48 bids A$30–180.
- [SPEND] AU BAS workbooks: Etsy ~US$11.24 (range $2.56–A$47); Payhip TinyBAS A$19; Whop BASReady A$29.99.
- [FACT] Etsy digital sales are winner-take-most: PrioriDigitalStudio 177,462 sales vs TrackerSpreadsheetCo 79,
  SmartSheetProv1 9. A third-party tracker found 44% of 146,271 Notion template listings earned $0.
- [FACT] Chrome MV2 extensions removed from Chrome Web Store 31 Aug 2026; orphaned extensions found were niche/free.
  Extension revenue reality: ExtensionPay devs collectively >$25k (Feb 2022); Flippa listing 871 users/$172 MRR;
  another 52 installs/£0 after 6 weeks; outlier Easy Folders $3,700 MRR.
- [FACT] Mailchimp free plan reduced to 250 contacts / 500 sends from 17 Feb 2026 — but many free alternatives exist.
