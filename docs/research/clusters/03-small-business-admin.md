## Key evidence

- [FACT] SBSCH: no new registrations after 1 Oct 2025; closed permanently from 1 Jul 2026 (ato.gov.au/sbsch, updated 5 Jan 2026).
- [FACT] Payday Super passed (Treasury release 4 Nov 2025); super must reach fund within 7 business days of payday from 1 Jul 2026.
- [FACT] Free/cheap substitutes filled the gap: AustralianSuper and HESTA free clearing houses; Brighter Super SuperChoice at
  no cost; Beam free in Microkeeper/foundU, included in QuickBooks Payroll; e-PayDay and freepay free for <4 staff; Payroller 99c/employee.
- [FACT] Intentional wage underpayment criminal from 1 Jan 2025; Voluntary Small Business Wage Compliance Code gives safe harbour.
- [FACT] Bank export limits: CommBank CSV ~600 recent transactions; ANZ 2 years, open accounts only; Xero direct PDF import is US/Canada only.
- [SPEND] "Aussie Bank Statement Converter" (AU, founded 2025) on TrustMRR: ~US$746 Stripe revenue last 30 days, ~US$1,515 all-time,
  MRR US$144 from 2 subscriptions (listing updated 9 Sep 2026). — trustmrr.com/startup/aussie-bank-statement-converter
- [SPEND] Converter prices: yourbankstatementconverter.com $15/400 pages; convertmybankstatement.com $10/20/40 per month;
  BankConv $2.99/document or $29–79/mo; DocuClipper ~US$20–159/mo; plus EntryRocket, StatementDesk, Parsefield,
  BankStatementLab, ZeraBooks, InvoiceDataExtraction.
- [SPEND] Freelancer.com statement-conversion jobs: A$5–250; average bids ~A$5/hour.
