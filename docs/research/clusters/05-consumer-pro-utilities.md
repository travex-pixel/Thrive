### Legal boundary (Migration Act 1958)
- s276(1) immigration assistance = using knowledge of migration procedure to prepare/help prepare/advise on a visa application.
- s280 offence for unregistered persons.
- s276(3) a person does NOT give immigration assistance if they *merely* do clerical work, translate/interpret,
  tell someone they must apply, or pass on third-party information without substantial comment.
- Home Affairs review fact sheet flags "immigration assistance disguised as clerical work" as a risk.
- [INFERENCE] File processing, indexing, verbatim quoting of Home Affairs checklists ≈ fine. Tailored gap-scoring,
  "is my evidence enough", or drafting relationship statements ≈ not fine. Get advice before launch.
