## Key regulatory facts (verify against primary sources before building)

- [FACT] NDIS Pricing Schedule 2026–27 replaced the old PAPL price guide from 1 July 2026; claiming
  rules remain in the 2025–26 PAPL. Standard Level 1 weekday daytime support worker cap A$73.58/hr
  (+~4.75%). DAAR counts 210 items up, 65 down, 106 new, 41 removed.
  — blog.daar.com.au/news/ndis-pricing-changes-2026-27 ; provider360.com.au/articles/ndis-pricing-schedule-2026-27/ ;
  carevo.com.au/blog/independent-support-worker-pay-rates
- [FACT] NDIS Amendment (Securing the NDIS for Future Generations) Act 2026 passed 19 Aug 2026.
  90-day claim window from 1 Dec 2026 for all management types (secondary sources); self-managers
  must keep records 3 years (NDIA Self-Management Advisory Group summary, Sep 2026).
  — ndiscommission.gov.au media release ; orangeplan.com.au/guides/ndis-90-day-claim-rule/ ;
  ndis.gov.au/news/11722-self-management-advisory-group-meeting-summary-september-2026
- [FACT] Mandatory registration for SIL providers and platforms from 1 July 2026; wider scope from July 2027 (details pending).
- [CONTESTED] 10% lower price limit for unregistered providers of some community participation supports from 1 Jan 2027.
- [FACT] SCHADS sleepover award variation effective first full pay period on/after 1 June 2026; FWO
  Disability Support Services Sector Inquiry; Yooralla EU (A$2.05m) and Sunnyfield (~A$5.4m, pay points, ~14 Oct 2026).
  — fairwork.gov.au sleepover changes page ; hcamag.com Sunnyfield article
- [FACT] Aged Care Act 2024 + Support at Home commenced 1 Nov 2025; price caps due 1 July 2026 deferred;
  single-provider model until at least June 2027; proof of delivery required.
  — servicesaustralia.gov.au/aged-care-reforms-start-1-november-2025 ; australianageingagenda.com.au (price caps deferred)
- [FACT] Trilogy Care auto-rejects common invoice errors since 15 Dec 2025 (itemisation, per-km transport,
  name/ABN/GST mismatches). — trilogycare.com.au/support-at-home-billing-changes-what-you-need-to-know
- [FACT] ~267,396 unregistered providers paid in June 2026 quarter (all types). — ndisgrowth.com.au/ndis-statistics/
