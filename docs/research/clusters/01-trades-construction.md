## Most decision-relevant evidence

- [FACT, against] **MakeSWMS (AI SWMS SaaS, founded Dec 2025): Stripe-verified ~$41 last 30 days, ~$40 MRR, 2 subscribers,
  $145 all-time** (TrustMRR listing updated 12 Jul 2026). — trustmrr.com/startup/makeswms-com
- [SPEND] SWMS market prices: QuickSWMS from $9.99/SWMS; SWMS Generator A$29/SWMS; Trak $20; Gumroad pack A$79/8;
  OneClickSWMS $60/mo; SiteBook $29/user/mo; CloudSWMS $119–258/mo (+ bundles via Bunnings); Worx Safety $239–449/mo.
- [FACT, against] Free SWMS generators/templates: AI4HSE (photo → SWMS), Plexa, Standardsmate (1 free/mo), SafeWork/MBAV/NT templates.
- [FACT] Federal Safety Commissioner: generic SWMS not made site-specific won't meet requirements.
- [FACT] VIC SOPA reforms effective 15 Apr 2026 (excluded amounts abolished; all reasons must be in schedule).
- [SPEND] Payapps for subbies $65/claim or $49–440/mo; SopaServe free tier; paymentclaims.ai.
- [FACT] Construction insolvencies 1,515 (2018-19) → 3,475 (2025-26) per Ai Group.
- [COMPLAINT] Test & tag app iCertifi rated 1.0 (data loss, subscription prompts); PATorganiser $19/mo; vendor software bundled with testers.
- [FACT] NSW written contract needed > $5,000 incl. GST; QLD > $3,300; QBCC: poorly recorded variations among the most common dispute causes.
- [FACT] VIC Domestic Building Contracts Amendment Act 2025 commencement deferred (reported 31 Mar 2027, unconfirmed).
